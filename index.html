Main character

Frank Evelyn

Quiet, sharp and loyal. At eighteen in Ibadan, one wrong choice costs him everything. Years later in Lagos, the past comes looking for him, and the only person he has left is his little sister, Anna.

  • 01Starts in Ibadan at 18. The main story begins in Lagos at 22.
