Main character

Frank Evelyn

Quiet, sharp and loyal. At eighteen in Ibadan, one wrong choice costs him everything. Years later in Lagos, the past comes looking for him, and the only person he has left is his little sister, Anna.

  • Starts in Ibadan at 18, the main story begins in Lagos at 22
  • Rises from small hustles to the top of the underworld
  • Every choice shapes how his story ends

The world

A compressed Nigeria: real directions, real neighbours, real landmarks, with Lagos as the biggest, most detailed city. Every place sounds like itself, down to the radio stations.
